## Publication text

Titles and result wording were checked on September 22, 2026 against arXiv metadata and the corresponding PDFs:

- **LiteResearcher:** [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17931v1), *LiteResearcher: A Scalable Agentic RL Training Framework for Deep Research Agent*. Author order continues to follow v1. Reported scores are 71.3% on GAIA-Text and 78.0% on Xbench-DS; the comparison is scoped to models evaluated in the paper, with the leading open-source result specified for Xbench-DS.
- **ST-Lite:** [arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00188v3), *ST-Lite: Training-Free KV Cache Compression with Spatio-Trajectory Guidance for Long-Horizon GUI Agents*. The abstract and Table 2 report up to 2.35× decoding speedup at fivefold KV cache compression. The previous 2.45× / +7.3% summary was removed because that combination was not supported by this version. All three equal-contribution markers are retained.
- **SAIL:** [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05380v2), *SAIL: Self-Amplified Iterative Learning for Diffusion Model Alignment with Minimal Human Feedback*.

Talk titles are independent presentation titles and are not rewritten to match publication titles.
